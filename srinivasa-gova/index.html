The Soul of Authentic Tamil Nadu Dairy

In an era of artificial milk powders and fast-track industrial cooking, Srinivasa Gova stands as a guardian of the timeless Tamil Nadu dairy tradition. We source pristine morning milk exclusively from indigenous country cows fed on natural green fodder.

Every batch is simmered slowly over wood fire in heavy bell-metal and brass urns (urulis) for over four hours until the natural sugars crystallise into a soft, amber, melt-in-your-mouth delicacy. We never compromise on purity: no gelatin, no synthetic stabilizers, and zero corn starch.
